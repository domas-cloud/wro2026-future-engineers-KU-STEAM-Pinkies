## Our Goal

Our goal this season was to build a compact autonomous self-driving robot that would be:

- mechanically stable,
- easy to control,
- strong in straight driving,
- accurate in turning,
- and reliable in obstacle obedience and parking.

From the beginning, we did not want to build a robot that only looked advanced. We wanted to build a robot that actually worked better in practice.

## Our Main Engineering Idea

One of the most important things we learned was that the best robot is not always the most complicated one.

Our previous robot was larger and used a more complicated steering concept. In practice, that brought more friction, more resistance, and worse repeatability. Because of that, this season we moved toward a smaller and simpler concept that gave us more control.

## Our Final Robot in One Short Description

Our final robot is a:

- compact **rear-wheel-drive** vehicle,
- with **front-wheel steering**,
- a **mechanical rear differential**,
- **servo-based steering**,
- **Raspberry Pi Zero** for perception,
- and **ESP32** for control and actuation.

Its approximate dimensions are:

- **Length:** 21 cm  
- **Width:** 10 cm  
- **Height:** 8 cm

We believe these dimensions are very close to ideal for our robot because they allow easier turning and are well suited for the parking task.
