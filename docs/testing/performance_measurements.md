# Measurements from Hardware V1

These are measurements we actually kept from the working first robot. They are here as a baseline for Hardware V2, not as claims about the rebuilt car.

## Straight drift

We drove the car 3 m and measured the side offset at the end.

| Run | Later V1 | Earlier version |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 cm | 11 cm |
| 2 | 5 cm | 10 cm |
| 3 | 3 cm | 12 cm |
| 4 | 4 cm | 9 cm |
| 5 | 4 cm | 11 cm |

Average: **4.0 cm** after the steering/sensor improvements versus **10.6 cm** earlier.

## Turning space

The early steering layout needed about **46 cm** for the same type of 90° turn. The later layout used about **39 cm**. We treated this as an approximate practical measurement, not laboratory accuracy.

## Repeated practice runs

| Test | Attempts | Clean/completed |
|---|---:|---:|
| open straight | 5 | 5 |
| obstacle slalom | 5 | 4 |
| full practice route | 5 | 4 |

The one slalom failure was a late correction near the second obstacle. In the full-route set, one run lost alignment after a tight corner.

These results supported the steering geometry, silicone front tyres, LEGO differential and rigid IMU mounting. V2 will repeat comparable tests after the new motor, PCB, battery and camera are installed.
