# Performance status

Hardware V2 does not have final performance results yet.

The working Hardware V1 baseline is recorded in [`../testing/performance_measurements.md`](../testing/performance_measurements.md): average 3 m drift improved from 10.6 cm to 4.0 cm, approximate 90° turning space improved from 46 cm to 39 cm, and the later practice sets produced 5/5 clean open-straight runs, 4/5 obstacle-slalom runs and 4/5 full-route completions.

We will add V2 results here only after the final motor, PCB, battery, camera and source are on the same car. The goal is a direct V1-versus-V2 comparison, not a best-run showcase.
