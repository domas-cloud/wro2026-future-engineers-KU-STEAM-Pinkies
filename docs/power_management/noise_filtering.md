# Noise Reduction And Filtering

## Noise Sources

- motor switching noise;
- servo load spikes;
- sensor signal fluctuations;
- shared-ground and wiring interference.

## Reduction Approach

- keep sensitive signal wires separate from high-current paths as much as possible;
- keep wiring short and organized;
- use filtering and regulation where needed;
- verify sensor stability after the full robot is powered on.

## Why This Matters

Noise can cause unstable steering or unreliable obstacle detection.
The documentation should show that this was considered during design, not only after a failure happened.
