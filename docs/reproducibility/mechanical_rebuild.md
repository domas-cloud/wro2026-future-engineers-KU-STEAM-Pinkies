# Mechanical Rebuild Status

## Version status

The previous Hardware V1 mechanical rebuild notes were archived at [`archivo/hardware-v1-esp32-250rpm/docs/reproducibility/mechanical_rebuild.md`](../../../archivo/hardware-v1-esp32-250rpm/docs/reproducibility/mechanical_rebuild.md).

The existing CAD/STL and photographs describe the current mechanical baseline. Hardware V2 retains that baseline provisionally, but the new motor, LiPo and PCB may require new mounts.

## Confirmed retained elements

- rear-wheel-drive layout;
- rear differential;
- front steering with MG90S;
- custom silicone front wheels;
- existing steering concept and available model files.

## Hardware V2 mechanical information still required

- exact final motor and mount;
- battery size and retention;
- PCB dimensions and mounting holes;
- PixyCam bracket and angle;
- sensor positions and heights;
- final chassis dimensions and mass;
- wheelbase, track widths, wheel diameter and ground clearance;
- cable routing and strain relief;
- updated CAD for every changed custom part.

## Required assembly order

The final guide should show:

1. chassis preparation;
2. rear differential and selected motor installation;
3. front steering assembly and MG90S centring;
4. wheel clearance check;
5. PCB, LiPo and connector mounting;
6. PixyCam and sensor alignment;
7. cable retention;
8. final measurements and photographs.

## Acceptance checks

- drivetrain rotates without binding;
- differential permits different rear-wheel speeds in a turn;
- steering returns repeatably to centre;
- wheels do not touch the chassis at the final limits;
- motor, battery and PCB cannot move during a run;
- camera and sensors retain alignment;
- all final dimensions are recorded.

Do not publish exact Hardware V2 assembly dimensions until they are measured.
