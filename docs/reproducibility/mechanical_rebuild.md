# Mechanical rebuild

The robot uses rear-wheel drive, front steering, a LEGO differential, MG90S steering servo, printed steering/drivetrain parts and custom silicone front tyres. CAD/STL files are in [`../../models/`](../../models/).

The V1 chassis was roughly 21 × 10 × 8 cm, but those numbers will be measured again after the V2 battery, PCB, camera and final motor are installed.

The final version of this page will add wheelbase, front/rear track, wheel diameters, ground clearance, exact motor/mount, steering centre/limits and photos showing how the parts are assembled.
